SEO & Keyword Research Strategy

A keyword research project designed to identify relevant search terms, understand search intent and suggest SEO-friendly content for Zar-e-Zeenat, a women's clothing brand.

About the Brand

Zar-e-Zeenat is a Pakistani women's clothing brand offering stylish and comfortable shalwar qameez, pants and shirts, abayas and office wear. The brand aims to attract online shoppers through search engine optimization, relevant keywords and helpful fashion content.

Project Goal

Find Relevant Keywords

Identify search terms related to women's fashion and the products offered by the brand.

Understand Search Intent

Understand what users want when they search for particular fashion-related keywords.

Suggest Content

Create SEO-friendly content ideas based on keywords and users' search needs.

Search Intent Analysis

Informational Intent

Users want information, tips or ideas.

Example: How to style shalwar qameez

Commercial Intent

Users are researching products before making a purchase.

Example: Elegant abayas for women

Transactional Intent

Users are ready or intending to buy a product.

Example: Buy abaya online Pakistan

Navigational Intent

Users want to find a specific brand or website.

Example: Zar-e-Zeenat clothing

Basic SEO Strategy

1. Keyword Optimization: Use relevant keywords naturally in titles, headings and website content.

2. Meta Description: Write clear and attractive meta descriptions for website pages.

3. Quality Content: Publish useful fashion guides, styling tips and product descriptions.

4. Image Optimization: Use descriptive image filenames and alt text for clothing images.

5. Mobile Friendly Website: Make the website responsive and easy to use on mobile devices.

6. Internal Linking: Connect related product pages and blog articles.

7. Search Engine Monitoring: Use Google Search Console to monitor website performance and search queries.

Expected Outcomes

Organic Visibility

Improve the website's visibility in search engine results.

Relevant Traffic

Attract visitors searching for women's fashion products.

Better Content

Develop content based on users' search intent.

Potential Sales

Encourage potential customers to explore products and make purchases.

Conclusion

Keyword research is an important part of search engine optimization. By identifying relevant keywords and understanding search intent, Zar-e-Zeenat can develop useful content and improve its online visibility. A basic SEO strategy focused on relevant keywords, optimized product pages, helpful blog articles and mobile-friendly design can support the brand's digital marketing goals.
